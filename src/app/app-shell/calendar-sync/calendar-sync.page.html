Calendar sync

Calendar sync is a personal synchronization service run by Joosep Kõivistik. It connects Google Calendar and Google Tasks with a private, self-hosted personal knowledge-management system, so the owner's own calendar entries and tasks can sit alongside their notes.

It serves one person, the owner. It is not a commercial product and it is not offered to the public.

The service uses Google data only to keep the owner's notes and Google account in step. It is not sold, it is not used for advertising, and it is not shared with third parties beyond what the privacy policy describes.

The full details, including how data is stored and how to revoke access, are in the privacy policy.
