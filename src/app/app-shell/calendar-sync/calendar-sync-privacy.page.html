Calendar sync

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

This policy covers Calendar sync, the personal synchronization service described on the Calendar sync page.

Who runs this service

Calendar sync is operated by Joosep Kõivistik as a personal, non-commercial project. It is not a company and has no customers. To ask a question about this policy or about data held by the service, contact the operator through their GitHub profile at github.com/equilerex ↗.

What Google data the service uses

With the owner's permission, the service accesses the owner's own Google Calendar events and Google Tasks through Google's APIs. It does not access other Google data. The service is used by one person, the owner, and is not open to other people's Google accounts.

Why it uses this data

The data is used only to provide synchronization. Calendar entries and tasks are copied into the owner's private notes. If the owner changes an entry in those notes, the service can write that change back to the owner's Google Calendar or Google Tasks. The data is not used for any other purpose.

How the data is used and shared

  • The data stays within the owner's private personal system.
  • Google user data is not sold.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, and not for building advertising profiles.
  • This service does not use Google user data to train or improve AI or machine-learning models.
  • Data is not shared with third parties except where the operation of the service requires it:
    • Obsidian Sync, which keeps the owner's notes in step across the owner's own devices. It is an end-to-end encrypted sync service.
    • AI assistants that the owner chooses to use on their own notes. When the owner asks an assistant to work with their notes, that assistant may read synced calendar and task entries.

How the data is stored and protected

Synced entries are kept in the owner's private notes. Those notes are held on a private server that the owner operates and on the owner's own devices. The Google access credentials are kept in a separate restricted file on that server, not in the notes. Access to the server and the notes is limited to the owner's accounts.

These are reasonable measures for a small personal project. No system can be guaranteed to be free of security problems, and the service has not been independently audited.

How long the data is kept

Synced entries stay in the owner's notes until the owner deletes them. The owner can delete them at any time. Deleting the stored Google credentials ends the service's access to the account.

How to revoke access

You can revoke this service's access to your Google Account at any time. Open myaccount.google.com/permissions ↗, choose the app, and remove its access. Once access is removed, the service can no longer read or update your Google Calendar or Google Tasks. Notes that were already synced remain until they are deleted. To ask for them to be deleted, contact the operator as described above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The service's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy ↗, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy is updated when the service's handling of data changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated.
